## Instructions

1. Get a scale for weighing the noodles
2. Turn the scale on, make sure the weight is set to ounces and is at zero ounces with nothing on it
3. Put the pasta bowl on the scale
4. Add noodles to the bowl until the scale says 8 ounces
5. Put a lid on the bowl
6. Put all the completed pasta bowls in the cooler
